Allergens & ingredients

Have a food allergy? Call 931-372-3122 before placing your order to discuss your allergy with our team. Removing a topping does not establish that a meal is free of that ingredient or safe from cross-contact.

Avoid assuming an item is allergen-free based on its name or the choices shown online. Call us before ordering to discuss the ingredients and preparation of the items you are considering. Online topping choices do not communicate an allergy request to the kitchen.

The nine major US food allergens are milk, eggs, fish, crustacean shellfish, tree nuts, peanuts, wheat, soybeans, and sesame. Other ingredients can also cause reactions. Read FDA food allergy guidance.

Ordering & payments

Review your selections, contact details, subtotal, tax, and total before submitting an order. Pickup times and kitchen stages are estimates updated by staff.

Online payment is confirmed only after the payment provider verifies it. If payment is uncertain, check its status before paying again. Pay-at-pickup orders remain unpaid until collected.

For order changes, cancellations, or refund requests, contact the restaurant directly. Cancelling an order does not automatically refund an online payment.

Cancellations & refunds

We want every family meal to be right. If there is a problem with your order, call 931-372-3122 so our team can help. Have your order number and a description of the issue ready. You can also email l7163747@gmail.com; call for time-sensitive changes.

Changes and cancellations. Contact us as soon as possible. We can cancel and refund an order that has not entered preparation. Once preparation has started, changes or cancellations depend on how far the order has progressed and cannot be guaranteed. Closing a payment page does not cancel an order.

Incorrect, missing, or unsatisfactory items. Please report the issue promptly, preferably on the day of pickup and within 24 hours, with your order number. We will review the issue and arrange an appropriate remake, replacement, or refund for the affected items. Photos may help us resolve the issue, but do not send sensitive payment information.

Orders we cannot fulfill. If we cancel your order or cannot provide an item you paid for, we will offer an agreed replacement or refund the unavailable items. If we cannot fulfill the order, we will refund the amount paid.

Missed pickups and preferences. Prepared orders that are not collected, changes of mind after preparation, and items prepared as ordered are not automatically refundable. Please contact us so we can review the circumstances.

Receiving a refund. Approved online refunds are returned through the original payment method. Processing time depends on the payment provider and your bank. An unpaid pay-at-pickup order has no online payment to refund. We will confirm a refund separately from an order cancellation.

This policy does not limit any rights or remedies required by applicable law.

Privacy & your information

This ordering service uses the contact details you supply to manage your account, fulfill orders, and send account and order emails. Order records include the items ordered, amounts, and payment status. Passwords are stored as hashes.

Online card details are entered on Stripe’s hosted checkout and are not stored in the restaurant’s database. Stripe processes online payment information; Google’s Gmail service delivers transactional emails.

Signing in uses a session cookie. Online checkout also stores a temporary access token in your browser to recover payment status. Keep receipt and tracking links private.

External fonts and images may connect your browser to their providers. For privacy questions or to request access, correction, or deletion of your account information, email l7163747@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity and retain transaction records required for payment, dispute, or legal obligations. Do not email passwords or card details.
