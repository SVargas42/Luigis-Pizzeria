    A little information before you order. Please review ingredients, payment details, and how your information is used.

    Allergens & ingredients

    Have a food allergy? Speak directly with the restaurant before placing your order. Removing a topping does not establish that a meal is free of that ingredient or safe from cross-contact.

    Avoid assuming an item is allergen-free based on its name or the choices shown online. This menu does not provide a verified ingredient-by-ingredient allergen chart.

    The nine major US food allergens are milk, eggs, fish, crustacean shellfish, tree nuts, peanuts, wheat, soybeans, and sesame. Other ingredients can also cause reactions. Read FDA food allergy guidance.

    Ordering & payments

    Review your selections, contact details, subtotal, tax, and total before submitting an order. Pickup times and kitchen stages are estimates updated by staff.

    Online payment is confirmed only after the payment provider verifies it. If payment is uncertain, check its status before paying again. Pay-at-pickup orders remain unpaid until collected.

    For order changes, cancellations, or refund requests, contact the restaurant directly. Cancelling an order does not automatically refund an online payment.

    Privacy & your information

    This ordering service uses the contact details you supply to manage your account, fulfill orders, and send account and order emails. Order records include the items ordered, amounts, and payment status. Passwords are stored as hashes.

    Online card details are entered on Stripe’s hosted checkout and are not stored in the restaurant’s database. Stripe processes online payment information; Google’s Gmail service delivers transactional emails.

    Signing in uses a session cookie. Online checkout also stores a temporary access token in your browser to recover payment status. Keep receipt and tracking links private.

    External fonts and images may connect your browser to their providers. For questions about your information or a request concerning your account, contact the restaurant directly.
